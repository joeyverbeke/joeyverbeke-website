Open Windows

Open Windows was a collaborative installation from Signal to Noise Media Labs, an interdisciplinary art & technology collective based in Denver, Colorado. The interactive light sculpture resided at the Denver International Airport for two years. Open Windows created a simple, elegant, and playful interaction between travelers through the dancing of responsive lights. Open Windows was created with a focus on harnessing interdisciplinary talents to allow the strengths of individuals to come together, with the goal of creating a novel piece of new media that straddles the disciplines of science and art.
